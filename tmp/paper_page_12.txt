5. Conclusion and future work
Inthispaper,ahybridmeta-heuristic,thehybridgenetic
tabu search algorithm (HGTSA) is proposed to solve
the flexible job shop scheduling problem (FJSP) with
the objective of minimising makespan, addressing the
demands of mass customisation in the modern business
environment. HGTSA combines the global search capa-
bilitiesofthegeneticalgorithm(GA)withthelocalsearch
strengths of the tabu search algorithm (TS), achieving a
balancebetweenexplorationandexploitation.
TheeffectivenessofHGTSAisdemonstratedthrough
comparisonswithfivestate-of-the-artoptimisationalgo-
rithms on three widely used FJSP benchmarks. Experi-
mental results demonstrate the significant advantages of
thealgorithm.
