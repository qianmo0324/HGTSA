In this paper, an efficient hybrid meta-heuristic
algorithm, a hybrid genetic tabu search algorithm
(HGTSA), is proposed for benchmarks of HFSP. Fea-
turing a well-designed framework and three innova-
tive operators, it can well balance the search depth
and computational efficiency. An efficient hybrid neigh-
bourhood structure is incorporated to enhance perfor-
mance.Comprehensiveexperimentsconductedonstan-
dard benchmarks validate the effectiveness of HGTSA.
Thealgorithmnotonlyshowsbettersolutionqualityand
stability than all the previous excellent algorithms, but
also improves the upper bound of the ‘06a’ instance on
DPdata benchmark.
